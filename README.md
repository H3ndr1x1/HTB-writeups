## Rules I follow

These follow HackTheBox's [writeup guidelines](https://help.hackthebox.com/en/articles/5188925-streaming-writeups-walkthrough-guidelines) and [platform rules](https://help.hackthebox.com/en/articles/12325897-hack-the-box-platform-rules):

- **Retired content only.** A writeup goes up only after HTB officially retires the machine. Nothing here covers active machines, challenges or Sherlocks.
- **No Pro Labs or Enterprise content.** These can't be published, apart from the few Mini Pro Labs HTB explicitly allows.
- **No flags.** `user.txt` and `root.txt` contents are never published.
- **Secrets redacted.** Passwords and hashes are partially masked.

## Disclaimer

Everything here was done in HackTheBox's legal lab environment and is for educational purposes only. Don't use these techniques against systems you don't have permission to test.
